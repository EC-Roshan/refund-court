# Quick-Commerce Customer Refund & Dispute Policy

This policy governs dispute adjudication in Refund Court. All advocates must cite specific rule numbers when presenting arguments.

---

### Rule 3.1: Missing Item Refund
- **Condition:** When an item is billed on the invoice but omitted from delivery, or when the warehouse packing scan confirms the item was never scanned into the order bag.
- **Remedy:** Full refund of the individual missing item's billed value to the customer's original payment method or wallet.

---

### Rule 3.2: Delivery Fee Retention
- **Condition:** Standard delivery fees are strictly non-refundable for order fulfillments, regardless of item disputes, unless the delivery delay exceeds 30 minutes beyond the promised delivery window.
- **Remedy:** Retain delivery fee for the business if actual delay is $\le 30$ minutes.

---

### Rule 3.3: Extreme Delivery Delay Goodwill Credit
- **Condition:** When the actual delivery duration exceeds the promised delivery duration by more than 30 minutes (actual delivery time - promised delivery time > 30 minutes).
- **Remedy:** 
  1. Full refund of the delivery fee (per Rule 3.2).
  2. A 20% goodwill credit calculated on the total billed order amount credited to the customer.

---

### Rule 3.4: Mandatory Photo Evidence for Scanned Items
- **Condition:** When the warehouse packing barcode scan logs confirm that all billed items were physically scanned into the dispatch parcel.
- **Remedy:** No refund or replacement may be issued on claims of "missing items" without verified customer photo evidence displaying the delivered bag, exterior shipping label, and unopened/opened tamper-evident security seal. Claims without photo evidence are rejected.

---

### Rule 3.5: High Refund Frequency & Fraud Review Threshold
- **Condition:** Any customer account recording more than 3 refund requests within the rolling past 30 days (`refunds_last_30_days > 3`).
- **Remedy:** Automated or discretionary refund approval is strictly suspended. The dispute is flagged as a potential abuse/fraud signal. Requires mandatory manual review, strict proof verification, and temporary account dispute limitation.

---

### Rule 3.6: Maximum Automatic Refund Cap
- **Condition:** The total aggregate refund value for any single dispute order.
- **Remedy:** Autonomous or advocate-recommended refunds are capped at 1,000 INR. Any dispute seeking relief exceeding 1,000 INR cannot be finalized at Tier-1 and requires escalated approval from the Support Lead or Operations Manager.
